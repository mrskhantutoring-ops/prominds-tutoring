How the ACT works

The ACT changed recently — it is shorter than the test many parents remember. Here is the current format, as published by ACT:

  • The composite score (1–36) comes from English, Math, and Reading. Science is now optional and scored separately.
  • The core test takes about 2 hours. Our classes teach every math topic step by step, so the format feels familiar on test day.
  • There is no penalty for guessing, and a calculator is allowed on the Math section.

Percents & word problems

A jacket originally priced at $84 is on sale for 25% off. A customer then uses a coupon for an additional $6 off the sale price. What is the final price of the jacket?

A. $57   B. $58.50   C. $63   D. $78

Show the worked solution

Take 25% off first: $84 × 0.75 = $63.

Then the coupon comes off the sale price: $63 − $6 = $57 (A)

Where students get stuck: the order. The percent discount applies to the price; the coupon applies after. Taking $6 off first gives $58.50 — answer B is that exact trap.

Modeling with equations

One phone plan charges a $20 monthly fee plus $0.08 per text message. A second plan charges a flat $32 per month with unlimited texts. For how many text messages do the two plans cost the same in a month?

A. 100   B. 150   C. 240   D. 400

Show the worked solution

Let t = the number of texts. Set the two costs equal: 20 + 0.08t = 32

Subtract 20: 0.08t = 12

Divide by 0.08: t = 150 (B)

Check: $20 + 150 × $0.08 = $20 + $12 = $32. ✓

Where students get stuck: knowing what to write down. "Cost the same" is the signal to set the two expressions equal — the classes teach students to spot that signal.

Functions

If f(x) = 3x² − 2x + 5, what is the value of f(−2)?

A. 5   B. 13   C. 21   D. 25

Show the worked solution

Substitute −2 everywhere, keeping the parentheses: 3(−2)² − 2(−2) + 5

(−2)² = 4, and −2(−2) = +4, so: 3(4) + 4 + 5 = 21 (C)

Where students get stuck: signs. Squaring −2 gives +4, and two negatives multiply to a positive. Dropping either sign lands on 13 — answer B.

Geometry — circles

A circle has a circumference of 24π inches. What is the area of the circle, in square inches?

A. 48π   B. 144π   C. 288π   D. 576π

Show the worked solution

Circumference: C = 2πr, so 24π = 2πr and r = 12.

Area: A = πr² = π(12)² = 144π (B)

Where students get stuck: 24 is the diameter's worth of π — the radius is half of it. Using 24 as the radius gives 576π, answer D.

Trigonometry

In right triangle ABC, angle C is the right angle and sin A = 5/13. If the hypotenuse is 26, what is the length of the side opposite angle A?

A. 5   B. 10   C. 12   D. 24

Show the worked solution

sin A = opposite / hypotenuse, so opposite = sin A × hypotenuse = (5/13) × 26 = 10 (B)

Bonus check: this is a 5-12-13 triangle scaled by 2 — the other leg is 24, which is why D sits there waiting.

Where students get stuck: picking the wrong side for sine. Sine always pairs the opposite side with the hypotenuse — never the adjacent side.
